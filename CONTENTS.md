**001. Six unit squares and \(S(6)=2\) (candidate).** Presents a computer-assisted candidate argument that six closed unit squares cannot cover a Euclidean square of side strictly greater than \(2\), i.e. \(S(6)=2\) or equivalently \(\Pi(2)=7\), addressing the conjecture of Dósa–Lángi–Tuza (arXiv:2601.16535). Ownership case analysis, normalisation, and joining-mode elimination are backed by machine certificates in an internal audit slice (`still_S=0`) and a partial Lean skeleton. ([Lean](lean/docs/001.md))
