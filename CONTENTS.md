**001. Six unit squares and \(S(6)=2\) (candidate, under revision).** Presents a computer-assisted candidate argument that six closed unit squares cannot cover a Euclidean square of side strictly greater than \(2\), i.e. \(S(6)=2\) or equivalently \(\Pi(2)=7\) (existential \(\Pi\); using known \(\Pi(2)\le 7\)), addressing the conjecture of Dósa–Lángi–Tuza (arXiv:2601.16535). Ownership case analysis, stepwise normalisation / reclassification, and joining-mode elimination are backed by machine certificates in an internal audit slice and a partial Lean skeleton whose Boolean flags are status metadata only.
