# S(6)=2 — Lean skeleton scope

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to:

- [Six unit squares cannot cover a square of side greater than 2](../../preprints/Six-unit-squares-cannot-cover-a-square-of-side-greater-than-2-October-21-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The shipped `S6Core` library provides a **partial** formalization skeleton for interfaces used in a computer-assisted **candidate** argument that \(S(6)=2\) (equivalently \(\Pi(2)=7\) with corrected \(\Pi\)).

In the shipped tree, obligations 1–4 are marked with human-maintained Boolean **status metadata** and compile with 0 `sorry` / extra axioms **in that skeleton**. This does **not** mean:

- the geometric theorem \(S(6)=2\) is proved in Lean;
- ownership / role classifiers match the manuscript (\(k\), \(F\ni O\), sixth tile);
- leaf goals of the form `living → True` exclude coverings;
- `reduction_to_standardForm` proves a concrete covering satisfies standard form (it constructs a fresh record with `true` fields).
