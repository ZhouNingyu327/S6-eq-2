# Geometric review obligations

These are the obligations of the proposed proof route, not assertions that
the listed implications have been formally certified.

## 1. Actual roles and finite classification

Check arbitrary real, closed unit squares, arbitrary overlap, and coverage of
every target point. Preserve duplicated midpoint hosts. The core now gives
the adjacent-host injection and all four residual host assignments explicitly.

## 2. G5 and the universal central box

Before invoking centre uniqueness for any inscribed target, verify all
G5 midpoint cases and the geometric inputs to the R8 arithmetic certificate.
The closed central box then follows from a finite disjoint closed partition
of a connected open box; it does not use a later root cap.

## 3. Local replacement and branch migration

The sole active rule is target-relative cover domination, followed by
reclassification where necessary. For each local replacement, record actual
retained points, hosts, chord states, and all output branches. Leaving an old
leaf is not itself a contradiction. The unused global one-shot scheme
supplies no premise here.

## 4. Centre-no-mid and axial caps

The centre-no-mid statement must hold universally before applying it to a
new inscribed target. Rechoose corner roles for that new target; do not
force the old F to retain its role. This prevents a circular use of R37.

## 5. Correct parallel pairing and tangency

TOP pairs with the central horizontal line; RIGHT pairs with the central
vertical line. Both actual intersections must be nonempty.
A singleton tangent is nonempty with length zero. Empty chords do not
satisfy the two-line hypothesis. Floating chords cannot be combined with
an adjacent chord by an unproved shared-starting-point maximum.

## 6. Far-end standard form

Account for all outer-edge gap states and all empty/nonempty horizontal
states of F. For the implication from F avoiding the target horizontal
segment to the whole F lying below it, use convexity together with the
diameter estimate which places the crossing inside that target segment.

## 7. Original points and the same bottom tile

Recover actual seam and exit points from the covering before applying
projection inequalities. A supporting-line intersection is not automatically
a point of C. The P_mu-to-P1 step uses convexity within the same C; it does
not rescale the target. Keep both bottom-gap endpoints and the lower axial
point in one actual F when using area or diameter.

## 8. Final contact and parameter domains

Check four membership alternatives, each allowed rotation sign, all angular
endpoints, and every denominator sign. A W-contains-QU estimate cannot be
moved to a right-bridge case without proving its hypothesis.
An endpoint substitution in a rational budget is analytic and does not
create a geometrically realizable endpoint layout.

## 9. Certificates and formalization

Independently check the encoded polynomials and domain cover, then separately
check that the real geometry implies that domain. A finite Boolean grid proves
only its combinatorial statement. Supporting Lean theorems and conditional
bridge statements are not an unconditional proof of the upper bound.

## 10. Editing and source fidelity

The detailed English modules were edited from source, with scoped notation
changes and explicit applicability qualifications. Formula syntax checks do
not establish preservation of every quantifier, negation or geometric
hypothesis. Selected source-to-English checks are documented separately.
Any found error must be traced to its affected implications and conclusions.
