Each certificate supports a specified implication chain:
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \text{actual cover}\ \Longrightarrow\
 \text{actual points and geometric premises}\\
 \Longrightarrow\ \text{closed parameter domain}\
 \Longrightarrow\ \text{certified sign}\ \Longrightarrow\ \bot.
\end{gathered}
\]
Arithmetic checks the sign on its encoded domain. It does not establish
the earlier geometric arrows. A contradiction in a model is useful only
after the actual cover is proved to imply that model.
Before multiplying a rational inequality by a denominator, prove its
sign on the actual domain. Before applying a two-line chord budget,
establish that both intersections are nonempty.
